faqs:
  - q: How do I know if my switchboard needs upgrading?
    a: Warning signs include ceramic fuses, no safety switches (RCDs), frequent tripping, or no physical room for new circuits. If you're adding solar, a battery or an EV charger, we assess it as part of the quote.
  - q: Are old ceramic fuse boards dangerous?
    a: They offer no protection against electric shock and are a common factor in house fires. Current standards require RCD protection on circuits — an upgrade brings your home up to standard.
  - q: How long does a switchboard upgrade take?
    a: Most residential upgrades are done in half a day to a day, with a short planned power outage while we change over.
  - q: Do you do upgrades on their own, without solar?
    a: Yes — we're a Registered Electrical Contractor (REC-32099) and take on standalone switchboard upgrades across our service area.

Your switchboard is the heart of your home's electrical system — and the first thing we look at before installing solar, a battery or an EV charger. Many Melbourne homes built before the 2000s are still running boards that can't safely take modern loads.


## Future-proofing, not just fixing

A good upgrade isn't just about today's job. We size and lay out the new board so a battery, backup circuit or EV charger can be added later without doing it all again — one upgrade, planned properly.
