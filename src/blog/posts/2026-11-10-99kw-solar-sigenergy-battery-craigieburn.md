Today's job: a 9.9 kWp system with a Sigenergy battery and essential-circuit backup on a single-storey home in Craigieburn.

{% youtube "dQw4w9WgXcQ", "9.9kW solar and Sigenergy battery installation in Craigieburn" %}

## What we did

- 22 × 450 W panels across the north and west faces — west array catches the evening peak
- Sigenergy SigenStor battery with backup module
- Essential circuits (fridge, lights, internet, garage) wired to the backup supply
- Switchboard tidy-up and labelling while we were in there

## What we learned

The west-facing array decision is worth a note: the customer works from home and uses most power from 3–7pm, so we traded a little total generation for a lot more self-consumption. Design from the bills, not the compass.

## The numbers at commissioning

System switched on at 2:40pm, immediately generating 6.1 kW under light cloud. Monitoring set up on the owner's phone before we left.
