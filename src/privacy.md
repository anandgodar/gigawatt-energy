Gigawatt Energy Pty Ltd ("we", "us") respects your privacy and handles personal information in accordance with the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

## What we collect

When you enquire or request a quote we collect the details you provide — typically your name, phone number, email address, suburb and information about your property and energy usage.

## How we use it

We use your information to respond to enquiries, prepare quotes, deliver and support our services, and process rebate applications you ask us to make on your behalf (for example with Solar Victoria). We do not sell your personal information.

## Storage and security

Your information is stored in the systems we use to run the business and is accessible only to our team. We take reasonable steps to protect it from misuse, loss and unauthorised access.

## Third parties

We share information with third parties only where needed to deliver your job — for example your electricity distributor for grid approval, or Solar Victoria for rebate processing — or where required by law.

## Access and contact

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information at any time by emailing [info@gigawattenergy.com.au](mailto:info@gigawattenergy.com.au) or calling 0451 644 220.
